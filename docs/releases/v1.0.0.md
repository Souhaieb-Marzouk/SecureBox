# v1.0.0 — Complete firewall + DNS protection

SecureBox 1.0.0 is the first stable release of the combined network-protection engine. **Local Firewall** and **DNS Shield** now run together in one on-device VPN: allowed traffic is forwarded, selected apps are dropped, and DNS is filtered through the signed blocklist and DoH.

## Highlights

### 🛡️ One VPN, both protections

- All apps are routed through one local `VpnService`.
- Allowed IPv4 TCP/UDP is relayed through protected sockets.
- Selected apps are blocked by UID: TCP reset, UDP dropped.
- DNS is filtered locally and forwarded over DoH to AdGuard, Cloudflare for Families or Quad9.
- Both switches can stay on at once; the service stops only when both are off.
- Works on phones, tablets and Android TV boxes without Private DNS.

### 📊 Protection statistics

- Ads, trackers, pages cleaned, dangerous links and locally blocked DNS queries.
- Persistent DataStore counters, reset from the page.
- DNS counters flush within about a second so a single block is visible.

### 🧠 Signed threat intelligence

- Feed rules, tracker prefixes and **domains**.
- Feed domains are enforced by the DNS filter and refresh at service start and every 15 minutes.
- Ed25519 verification before anything is stored.

### 📺 TV and accessibility

- D-pad focus anchors and scroll stops across every screen.
- TalkBack walkthrough passed on a physical phone.
- Large-font layouts verified up to the maximum system font; Settings’ protection card stacks cleanly.

### 🔁 Reboot and recovery

- DNS Shield keeps its persisted state across reboots and upgrades.
- The boot receiver and app-start path restore the VPN.
- Quick off/on retries tunnel establishment and no longer reports a false “Another VPN is active”.

## Verified on hardware

- **Phone:** combined firewall + DNS, blocked app, allowed app, DNS/feed blocking, statistics, HTTPS/download/upload, update check, feed update, bandwidth, ports, reboot, onboarding, TalkBack, maximum font.
- **Android TV box:** both protections, blocked app, allowed apps, DNS blocking, statistics, D-pad navigation.

## Privacy

SecureBox relays allowed app traffic **on-device** through protected sockets. It is not sent to a SecureBox server, not stored, and TLS is not intercepted. There is no analytics or advertising SDK.

## Known limits

- **IPv6 is deferred.** The tunnel is IPv4-only; IPv6 traffic is not filtered.
- Per-app blocking depends on UID attribution; unknown UIDs are allowed.
- Apps with their own DoH/DoT resolver can bypass DNS filtering.
- HTTPS is not intercepted.
- YouTube in-stream ads cannot be removed by DNS or an embedded browser.
- On some OEM builds `protect()` returns `false`; SecureBox binds the socket to a non-VPN network and continues.
- ColorOS may keep the package stopped after reboot; enable Android **Always-on VPN** for automatic start, or open the app to restore it.
