# v0.9.7 — Reboot/toggle and large-font fixes

Fix for the four issues found during the v0.9.6 phone/TV QA:

1. Settings → Protection card compressed its body text at 200% font.
2. DNS Shield showed as off after a reboot/app upgrade even though it was enabled.
3. Toggling both protections off and immediately on failed with “Another VPN is active”.
4. DNS Shield would not re-enable until the phone was rebooted.

## Fixes

- The firewall card now stacks **Configure in Network** below the description instead of sharing a row.
- The DNS Shield switch reflects the **persisted setting**, not just whether the service is currently running.
- Opening the DNS screen restarts the VPN when a persisted protection is enabled but the service is missing.
- A boot receiver restarts the combined VPN after reboot when either protection is enabled.
- Service establishment retries up to three times after a quick off/on toggle.
- SecureBox ignores its own transient VPN network for five seconds after shutdown, so it no longer shows a false “Another VPN is active” banner or blocks its own restart.

## Test

- Set maximum font size; Settings → Protection card must not compress.
- Reboot with DNS Shield on; the switch must remain on and the VPN must return.
- Turn both off, confirm internet, then turn both on; both must stay on and internet must work.
- 200% font/TalkBack checks from v0.9.6 remain valid.
