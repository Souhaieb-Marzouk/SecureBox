## Defect found during this pass

**SB-061 — switching to "Android TV box" on a phone trapped the user.** The 280dp sidebar left no
usable content and the Settings control to switch back was unreachable. Fixed in 0.4.1:

- compact mode under 600dp uses a 96dp icon rail and 12dp content padding;
- a **"Use phone layout"** button appears whenever the TV shell runs on non-TV hardware;
- `MainViewModel.usePhoneLayout()` persists `DeviceMode.PHONE`.
